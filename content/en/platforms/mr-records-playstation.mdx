## What MR. RECORDS Actually Is

According to the official Steam description, MR. RECORDS is an uplifting adventure built around a simple premise. George, an old dreamer, runs the record shop he always wanted. Every vinyl he plays transports him into a world of memories and emotion. In those worlds, you jump, slide, and move through psychedelic landscapes in time with the music. He is accompanied by his dog, Buzz.

That description tells you three things worth internalizing:

- **It is a rhythm adventure, not a pure rhythm game.** The store page frames movement through musical worlds as the core loop, alongside a colorful cast of customers and stories. That suggests a narrative frame around the music sections rather than a song-select screen with nothing in between.
- **The record shop is a hub, not a menu.** Vinyl functions as the gateway into each memory world, which implies the shop is where you meet customers and choose what to play.
- **Buzz is a named companion.** The dog is called out by name in the store description, so expect him to be present in the moment-to-moment experience rather than a background detail.
