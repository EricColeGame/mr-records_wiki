## Why "rhythm adventure" is a meaningful label

The phrase "rhythm adventure" is doing real work here. A pure rhythm game usually abstracts movement into a lane or a note chart. An adventure implies spatial exploration, characters, and progression. The official description supports both halves: George runs a shop (a narrative, character-driven frame) and each vinyl drops him into a world of memories where he moves in time with music (the rhythm layer).

The practical implication is that timing likely matters for traversal, not just for hitting notes. "Jump, slide, and move in time with the music" suggests the environment and the beat are linked, so a mistimed jump could mean a missed platform rather than a dropped combo. That is a reasonable reading of the advertised text, but it is an interpretation, not a documented mechanic. The official page does not describe the failure state, the scoring, or whether there is one at all.
