## Reading the Buzz Honestly

Player experience varies wildly in the first weeks after any showcase. Some people will call Mr Records the best thing they saw; others will shrug. Both reactions can be sincere.

What separates useful discussion from noise is specificity. "The art is great" tells a developer nothing. "The second area's lighting made the puzzle solutions hard to read against the background" tells them exactly where to look.

A few habits help here:

- Separate what you saw from what you assume.
- Check whether a claim comes from the developers or from a reposted clip.
- Give a project time to iterate before writing it off.
- Remember that a showcase segment is marketing, not a review.

Treating community reports as a starting point rather than a verdict keeps expectations realistic and keeps the conversation useful for everyone involved.
