## The Rhythm Adventure Hook: Why the Vinyl Framing Matters

Genre labels are cheap, so it's worth taking the "rhythm adventure" tag seriously. The description positions music as both the mechanic and the narrative device: you move *in time with the music*, and each track is a *memory*. Those two ideas pull in the same direction.

In a conventional rhythm game, the song is a challenge to overcome. In MR. RECORDS, the advertised framing suggests the song is a place to visit — a character's memory rendered as a psychedelic landscape you jump and slide through. If that structure holds, the emotional payoff and the mechanical demand are supposed to arrive together, which is a harder trick to land than a standard note chart.

Whether it works is unknowable until release. What's documented is the intent. What isn't documented is how tightly the rhythm element gates progress — for example, whether missing notes fails a run, or whether the music is more of a guiding tempo than a pass/fail system. Glee-Cheese Studio hasn't detailed that publicly.
