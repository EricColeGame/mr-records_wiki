## What MR Records Is, According to the Official Steam Listing

The Steam store page for [MR Records](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3053470/) (app ID 3053470) describes an uplifting adventure built around music and memory. You play as George, an older character who has fulfilled a long-held ambition by running the record shop he always wanted. Each vinyl record he puts on becomes a doorway: the music carries him into a world of memories and emotion, and he moves through those memories rather than simply listening to them.

Two details from the store description are worth holding onto because they define the game's shape:

- The memories are presented as psychedelic landscapes, not realistic recreations of a shop or a home.
- The core action is physical — jumping, sliding and moving through those landscapes in time with the music.

That combination places MR Records in the rhythm-platformer space rather than in pure rhythm games, where you tap along to a track without navigating a space. The store page also notes "a colourful cast of customers and stories," which suggests the shop itself is a hub where characters pass through, and the vinyl they bring or request connects to the memory worlds George visits.
