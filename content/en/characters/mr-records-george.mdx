## What MR. RECORDS Actually Is

MR. RECORDS is described on Steam as an uplifting adventure built around music and memory. George is not a fighter or a hero in the usual sense. He is an old dreamer running the record shop he always wanted, and the shop is the frame for everything else.

The central loop, as advertised, works like this: George plays a vinyl, and that vinyl transports him into a world of memories. Inside those worlds he jumps, slides, and moves through psychedelic landscapes in time with the music. He is helped by his dog Buzz, and the shop is populated by a colourful cast of customers with their own stories.

That combination matters for how you should think about the game. It is not a record-shop simulator with a rhythm minigame bolted on, and it is not a pure rhythm game with a shop menu between songs. The store page presents the two halves as one continuous idea: the shop gives the memories a home, and the memories give the shop its purpose.
