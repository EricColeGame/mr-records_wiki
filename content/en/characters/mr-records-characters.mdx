## The Confirmed Premise of MR. RECORDS

According to the official store description, you play as George, an old dreamer who runs the record shop he always wanted. Each vinyl he plays transports him into a world of memories. Inside those memories, you jump, slide and move through psychedelic landscapes in time with the music, accompanied by his dog, Buzz.
